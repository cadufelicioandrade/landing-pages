3. Health and wellness data

LevivaFast lets you record health and wellness information about yourself. You enter this data manually and it is entirely optional — the App works without any of it. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Fasting — fasting protocol, start and end times, fasting history and streaks
  • Body — weight, height, gender, body mass index, body fat percentage, and body measurements such as waist, hips, chest, arms, thighs, calves, neck, shoulders, forearms and wrists
  • Hydration — daily water intake
  • Electrolytes — sodium, potassium and magnesium intake
  • Health records — blood glucose, blood ketones, blood pressure (systolic and diastolic), heart rate and sleep duration
  • Symptoms — self-reported observations such as dizziness, weakness or tremors, and free-text notes you write

We treat all of the above as sensitive data. Specifically:

  • Health and wellness data is never used for advertising, never shared with advertising networks, and never used to build marketing or advertising profiles.
  • Health and wellness data is never sold and is never shared with data brokers.
  • It is stored on your device, and synced to your private cloud space only if you sign in.
  • It is used only to display your own history, charts and statistics back to you inside the App.

LevivaFast is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Any values, ranges or alerts shown in the App are for personal tracking and informational purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your health or beginning a fasting program.

4. Where your data is stored

Your records (fasting sessions, measurements, health entries, reminders and settings) are stored locally on your device in an app-private database. If you choose to sign in, the same data is also synced over an encrypted connection to Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication), so that you can restore it on another device or after reinstalling. Your cloud records are scoped to your account and are not readable by other users.

5. Why we process your data

Where the GDPR, the Brazilian LGPD or a similar law applies, our legal bases are:

  • Performance of a contract — providing the App's core tracking features and delivering the Premium subscription you purchased.
  • Consent — cloud sync, personalized ads, and any health data you choose to enter. You may withdraw consent at any time by signing out, disabling the feature, or deleting the data.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting and aggregate usage analytics used to keep the App stable and improve it.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We use the following third-party services, each subject to its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore, Analytics and Crashlytics — Google's privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — ads shown in the free version — Google's advertising policy
  • iOS Apple — Sign in with Apple and In-App Purchase processing for subscriptions — Apple's privacy policy
  • Android Google Play Billing — processing of subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your payment card details.

7. Advertising and tracking

The free version of LevivaFast displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Users with an active LevivaFast Premium subscription do not see ads.

iOS Before any tracking for personalized advertising takes place, the App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this decision at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android Ads may use your advertising ID for personalization, subject to your Android privacy settings, including the Privacy Sandbox / Topics API where available. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings, under Privacy > Ads.

We do not share your health, fasting, body or symptom data with any advertising network.

8. Subscriptions and purchases

LevivaFast Premium is offered as an auto-renewable subscription. Your subscription status is linked to your store account and is used only to unlock premium features.

iOS Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Android Payment is charged to your Google Play account. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions.

9. Your rights and choices

At any time, you may:

  • Use LevivaFast fully offline, without signing in to any account;
  • Sign out of your Google or Apple account in the App's Settings, which stops cloud sync;
  • Access, correct or delete any individual record directly in the App;
  • Request a copy of the personal data associated with your account;
  • Withdraw your consent to cloud sync or to personalized advertising;
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all data stored locally on the device.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to processing, to request data portability, or to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, write to cadufelicioandrade@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

10. Deleting your account and data

If you created an account by signing in, you can delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the App, in Settings > Account > Delete account. This is permanent and removes your records from our cloud storage. Deleting the App from your device removes the local copy.

You can also request deletion by writing to cadufelicioandrade@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

11. Data retention

Your records are kept for as long as your account exists, so that your history stays available to you. When you delete your account, your cloud records are deleted. Crash reports and aggregate analytics are retained by Firebase according to Google's default retention windows and are not linked to your health data.

12. Security

Data in transit is protected with TLS encryption. Cloud data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure and access is restricted by security rules so that records can be read and written only by the account they belong to. Local data is stored in the App's private storage area, which other apps on your device cannot read. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children

LevivaFast is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Intermittent fasting is not appropriate for children, and the App is intended for adults.

14. International transfers

Kodaru System operates from Brazil, and Google Firebase stores data on servers that may be located in other countries, including the United States. By using cloud sync, you understand that your data may be transferred and processed outside your country of residence, under safeguards provided by Google's data processing terms.
