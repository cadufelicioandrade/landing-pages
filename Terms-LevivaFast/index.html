1. Description of the service

LevivaFast is a personal intermittent fasting and wellness tracking application. It lets you choose a fasting protocol, start and track fasting windows, and record information about yourself including weight, body measurements, water intake, electrolytes, blood glucose and ketones, blood pressure, heart rate, sleep and self-reported symptoms. It presents that information back to you as history, charts and statistics, and can send reminder notifications. Cloud sync and backup are optional and require signing in.
