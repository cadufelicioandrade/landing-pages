3. Health and reproductive data

Cyvani lets you record health and reproductive information about yourself. You enter this data manually and it is entirely optional — the App works without any of it. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Cycle — period start and end dates, cycle length, predicted period, fertile window and ovulation estimates
  • Flow — daily flow intensity and spotting
  • Symptoms and mood — self-reported symptoms, mood, energy and free-text notes you write
  • Fertility signs — basal body temperature, cervical mucus, cervical position, and ovulation or pregnancy test results
  • Sexual activity — occurrences you choose to log and contraception method
  • Body and self-care — breast self-examination records and lifestyle entries such as sleep, water, activity and stress
  • Life stages — pregnancy and menopause information, when you enable those modes
  • Appointments — medical appointments and reminders you create

We treat all of the above as sensitive data. Specifically:

  • Cycle, fertility, sexual activity, pregnancy and any other health data is never used for advertising, never shared with advertising networks, and never used to build marketing or advertising profiles.
  • This data is never sold and is never shared with data brokers, insurers or employers.
  • It is stored on your device, and synced to your private cloud space only if you sign in.
  • It is used only to display your own history, predictions and statistics back to you inside the App.

Cyvani is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It must not be used as a method of contraception. Predictions for periods, ovulation and fertile windows are statistical estimates based on the data you entered and may differ from reality. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your health.

4. Where your data is stored

Your records (cycles, daily logs, symptoms, fertility signs, appointments, reminders and settings) are stored locally on your device in an app-private database. If you choose to sign in, the same data is also synced over an encrypted connection to Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication), so that you can restore it on another device or after reinstalling. Your cloud records are scoped to your account and are not readable by other users.

5. Why we process your data

Where the GDPR, the Brazilian LGPD or a similar law applies, our legal bases are:

  • Performance of a contract — providing the App's core tracking features and delivering the Premium purchase you made.
  • Consent — cloud sync, personalized ads, and any health or reproductive data you choose to enter. You may withdraw consent at any time by signing out, disabling the feature, or deleting the data.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting and aggregate usage analytics used to keep the App stable and improve it.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We use the following third-party services, each subject to its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore, Analytics and Crashlytics — Google's privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — ads shown in the free version — Google's advertising policy
  • RevenueCat — validation of purchases and subscription status — RevenueCat's privacy policy
  • iOS Apple — Sign in with Apple and In-App Purchase processing — Apple's privacy policy
  • Android Google Play Billing — processing of purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your payment card details.

7. Advertising and tracking

The free version of Cyvani displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Users with an active Cyvani Premium purchase do not see ads.

iOS Before any tracking for personalized advertising takes place, the App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this decision at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android Ads may use your advertising ID for personalization, subject to your Android privacy settings, including the Privacy Sandbox / Topics API where available. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings, under Privacy > Ads.

We do not share your cycle, fertility, sexual activity, pregnancy or symptom data with any advertising network.

8. Subscriptions and purchases

Cyvani Premium is offered as an auto-renewable monthly or yearly subscription, and also as a one-time lifetime purchase. Your purchase status is linked to your store account and is used only to unlock premium features.

iOS Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

Android Payment is charged to your Google Play account. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions.

9. Your rights and choices

At any time, you may:

  • Use Cyvani fully offline, without signing in to any account;
  • Sign out of your Google or Apple account in the App's Settings, which stops cloud sync;
  • Access, correct or delete any individual record directly in the App;
  • Request a copy of the personal data associated with your account;
  • Withdraw your consent to cloud sync or to personalized advertising;
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all data stored locally on the device.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to processing, to request data portability, or to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, write to contact@kodarysystem.com. We respond within 30 days.

10. Deleting your account and data

If you created an account by signing in, you can delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the App, in Settings > Account > Delete account. This is permanent and removes your records from our cloud storage. Deleting the App from your device removes the local copy.

You can also request deletion by writing to contact@kodarysystem.com from the email address associated with your account. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

11. Data retention

Your records are kept for as long as your account exists, so that your history stays available to you. When you delete your account, your cloud records are deleted. Crash reports and aggregate analytics are retained by Firebase according to Google's default retention windows and are not linked to your health data.

12. Security

Data in transit is protected with TLS encryption. Cloud data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure and access is restricted by security rules so that records can be read and written only by the account they belong to. Local data is stored in the App's private storage area, which other apps on your device cannot read. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children

Cyvani is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Minors who use the App should do so with the awareness and supervision of a parent or guardian, as required by local law.

14. International transfers

Kodaru System operates from Brazil, and Google Firebase stores data on servers that may be located in other countries, including the United States. By using cloud sync, you understand that your data may be transferred and processed outside your country of residence, under safeguards provided by Google's data processing terms.
